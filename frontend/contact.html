Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the calculations?

+
Our calculations are based on real market data from Sri Dungargarh, updated regularly to reflect current prices and costs. We source data from local suppliers, real estate agents, and business owners. However, actual costs may vary based on your specific location and circumstances.

Is BizCalc completely free?

+
Yes! BizCalc is completely free for all entrepreneurs in Sri Dungargarh. We don't charge any subscription fees, hidden charges, or premium versions. All features are available to everyone.

Can I export my calculations?

+
Currently, you can view and take screenshots of your calculations. You can also use the detailed breakdown directly for your bank applications and business plans. We're working on PDF export functionality.

Do banks accept BizCalc reports?

+
Yes! Our detailed, itemized breakdowns are prepared in the format that banks and financial institutions expect. Many entrepreneurs have successfully used BizCalc calculations in their loan applications.

How many business types do you support?

+
We currently support 13+ popular business types including retail shops, restaurants, cafes, pharmacies, electronics stores, jewelry shops, and more. If you need calculations for a business type we don't cover, please contact us.

Can I save my calculations?

+
Your calculations are computed instantly. Take screenshots or use your browser's print function to save the results. We're developing cloud-save functionality for registered users in the future.

What if land prices change?

+
We update our land price database regularly as market conditions change. You can always recalculate with current prices, and we recommend using the most recent calculations for loan applications and business planning.

Is my data secure?

+
We don't store any of your personal or calculation data on our servers. All calculations are done locally in your browser. Your data stays completely with you, and we maintain strict privacy standards.
