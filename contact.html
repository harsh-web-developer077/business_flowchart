Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are your calculations?

Our calculations are based on current market data from Sri Dungargarh. We update our data regularly to ensure maximum accuracy. However, actual costs may vary based on specific circumstances.

Is the calculator free to use?

Yes! BizCalc is completely free to use. We believe entrepreneurs should have access to accurate financial data without cost barriers.

Can I export my calculations?

Currently, you can take screenshots of your results. We're working on adding export features for PDF and Excel formats soon.

What if my business isn't listed?

We're continuously adding new business types. Please reach out via contact form to suggest businesses you'd like us to include.

How often is the data updated?

We update our market data quarterly to reflect changing conditions in Sri Dungargarh. Major updates are made immediately when significant changes are detected.

Can I use this for loan applications?

Yes! Many of our users have used BizCalc calculations as supporting documents for bank loan applications. Our detailed breakdowns help demonstrate financial planning.
